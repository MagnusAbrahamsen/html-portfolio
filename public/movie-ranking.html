The best anime list

My top 5 anime

Naruto

+: Chilhood classic, very good story. Gets better and better the more you watch. A lot of episodes
-: Not good animation for the current time(at beggining), can be a lot of time waste before fights or just to much talking sometimes

jujutsu kaisen

+: Very good animation, good story, cool characters, can kill of your favorite character
-: Can kill of your favorite character, can be confusing at times(like if you missed an episode, but they will explain later)

Death note

+: Very creative story, cool consept of MC, fun to not know who to root for.
-: Not the best animation, might have ended too soon

Dragon ball Z

+: OG anime, good fights, fun to know its all about the fights, fun with over powered MC, good world building.
-: Can be slow at times, sometimes fights are dragged out, a lot of the talking forever before fighting/ just staring at the enemy.

Solo leveling

+: A lot like the new dragonball, but better animation, less dragged out fights, cool consept of MC powers, fun watch.
-: Story is kinda just to make him stronger, can feel shallow at times.
